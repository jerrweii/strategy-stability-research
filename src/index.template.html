02 / What changed

Two workloads, two cadences

Fixed-parameter checks are cheap enough to schedule nightly in this small benchmark. Optimization is materially slower and heavier, so it moves to weekly, monthly, on-demand, or bring-your-own compute.

The untouched OOS replay contained only $OOS_TRADES trades. It proves the export/replay path, not robustness.
